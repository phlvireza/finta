The difference you feel first

Your budget resets on payday. Not on the 1st.

Almost every finance app slices your life into calendar months. If you get paid on the 25th, that split is wrong in a way that quietly ruins the numbers — your salary lands in one month while the rent, groceries and transport it pays for land in the next.

A calendar month
Salary lands
August

The paycheque is already a week old when the window opens — so August shows a month of spending against almost no income.

A Finta pay period
Salary lands
25 Jul – 24 Aug

The window opens the day the money arrives and closes the day before the next one. One paycheque in, one paycheque's spending out.

25 Jul 1 Aug 25 Aug

Set your payday once, during setup. From then on the entire app runs on that cycle — the dashboard, every budget, the analytics, the health score and the recap all use the same window.

  • Any day, 1 to 31. Pick the 31st and February still works — the anchor clamps to the last day of short months instead of spilling into the next one.
  • Periods never overlap or leave gaps. Each one ends the day before the next payday, whatever number you chose.
  • Step backwards any time. The period selector sits where the app title normally would, so last cycle is one tap away.
  • Income and the spending it funds stay together, which is the whole reason savings rate and the health score mean anything.

Privacy by construction

There is no server to trust.

Most finance apps ask you to hand over your spending history and then promise to look after it. Finta removes the step where you have to believe anyone. Your data never goes anywhere, because the app has nowhere to send it.

One file, on your device

Everything is a single SQLite database in the app's private storage. No copy exists anywhere else unless you make one yourself.

Hide every amount instantly

One tap on the eye icon masks every number in the app at once — for handing your phone to someone, or just reading it on a crowded train.

Leave whenever you like

Back up to a zip you keep, or export to CSV and open it in a spreadsheet. There is no lock-in to escape from.

How you can check

"No tracking" is easy to claim, so here is the version you can check yourself: turn on airplane mode and use Finta all day. Everything still works, because nothing was ever being sent anywhere. The app was built without the part that lets an app talk to the internet at all — so there is nothing quietly reporting what you tap, and nothing calling home when something goes wrong. The insights, the unusual-spending warnings and the health score are sums and averages worked out on your phone. The only time your data moves is when you tap share on a backup or an export — and then it goes exactly where you send it.

On-device intelligence

It notices things you never told it.

Finta reads your own history and points out what changed — without any of it leaving the phone.

  • Finds subscriptions by itself. When the same merchant charges you roughly the same amount roughly every month, Finta spots it and offers to start tracking it in one tap.
  • Flags an unusual amount as you type it. If a coffee costs ten times what your coffees normally cost, it asks before saving — and tells you what typical looks like. A nudge, never a block.
  • Scores your financial health out of 100 from your savings rate, how closely you hold your budgets, and how steady your income is — with all three sub-scores shown, not one mystery number.
  • Names your fastest-growing category before it becomes a surprise at the end of the cycle.

Built for real amounts

Logging a transaction takes three taps.

People abandon finance trackers because entry is tedious. So entry is the part Finta works hardest on.

  • A keypad built for six-digit numbers, with a 000 key — because typing 1.250.000 on a phone keyboard is how a habit dies.
  • Do maths in the amount field. Type 12000+3500 or 45000/3 and it just works. Splitting a bill needs no calculator app.
  • Merchant autocomplete that learns. Start typing a place you have been before and the account fills itself in.
  • One-tap favourites for the things you buy constantly, plus duplicate, and bulk edit for when you catch up on a whole week at once.

Budgets that tell the truth

"85% used" means nothing without a date.

Being 85% through your food budget is alarming on day 3 and completely fine on day 27. Every Finta budget bar carries a pace marker showing how far through the period you actually are, so the number has context.

  • A budget can renew itself. Switch on "Repeat every period" and the same limit comes back each cycle — monthly ones on your payday, not the 1st. Leave it off and it covers only the period you set it in.
  • Then unspent money carries over. Pick whether a quiet month tops up the next one, or whether overspending eats into it too.
  • You get told at 75% and again at 100%, right when you save the transaction that crossed the line.

Questions worth asking

Does it connect to my bank?

No — and that is deliberate. Bank connections require handing your credentials to a third-party aggregator that then holds your full transaction history. Finta doesn't have a server, so it couldn't do that even if it wanted to. You enter transactions yourself, or import a CSV your bank already gives you.

Do I have to set my budgets up again every month?

Only if you want to. Every budget has a "Repeat every period" switch — turn it on and the same limit comes back each cycle, renewing on your payday rather than the 1st, carrying unspent money across if you asked for that. Leave it off and the budget covers just the period you made it in, then moves to an "Ended" list instead of disappearing. It is a switch rather than an assumption because a budget you deliberately chose to repeat is one you can still explain to yourself six months later.

What happens if I lose my phone?

This is the honest trade-off of a local-first app: if the only copy is on the phone and the phone is gone, the data is gone. Finta gives you a one-tap backup that produces a zip file you can put in your own cloud drive, email to yourself, or keep anywhere you like. Do it occasionally and you are covered — on your terms, in a file you control.

Can I sync between two devices?

Not automatically. Sync means a server, and a server means your spending history sits on someone else's computer — which is the exact thing this app is built to avoid. You can move data between devices with a backup file whenever you need to.

Which platforms will it be on?

Android and iOS at launch. Join the waiting list and you'll hear once, on the day it's available.

Will it cost anything?

Pricing isn't settled yet. What is settled: no ads, no analytics, and no selling data — none of which are possible in an app that doesn't make network requests.

Can I get my data out?

Any time, two ways. A full backup is a zip containing the actual database file, and CSV export gives you a plain spreadsheet of every transaction. Both go straight to your share sheet.
